In the web and desktop apps, Zulip automatically uses your device's time
zone to display dates and times. You can choose a time zone in your profile
to use it for display and to share your local time on your
[user card](/help/user-cards) and [profile](/help/view-someones-profile).

## Choose a time zone

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/profile" />

      1. Under **Profile**, select a **Time zone** or choose **Automatic (device time zone)**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Choose a calendar and font

In automatic mode, Zulip displays dates in the Jalali calendar and uses
Vazir for Persian text when the active time zone is Tehran. In other time
zones it uses the Gregorian calendar and Zulip's standard font.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Desktop/Web">
    <FlattenedSteps>
      <NavigationSteps target="settings/preferences" />

      1. Under **General**, select a **Calendar** or **Font**.
    </FlattenedSteps>
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>
