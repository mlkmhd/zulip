---
title: Switch between classic and modern interfaces
---

import FlattenedSteps from "../../components/FlattenedSteps.astro";
import NavigationSteps from "../../components/NavigationSteps.astro";

Zulip starts in the classic interface and offers to switch to the modern
interface in a banner at the top of the page. Choose **Switch to modern** or
**Keep classic**. Zulip saves the choice to your account.

<FlattenedSteps>
  <NavigationSteps target="settings/preferences" />

1. Under **General**, select **Classic** or **Modern** in the **Web interface**
   dropdown. Zulip reloads to apply your choice.
    </FlattenedSteps>
